=== ConsentFlow Cookie Consent ===
Requires at least: 5.8
Requires PHP: 7.4
Stable tag: 0.1.0
License: GPLv2 or later

Cookie banner, automatic tracker blocking, geo rules and Google Consent Mode v2, managed from your ConsentFlow dashboard.

== Installation ==
1. Copy the `consentflow` folder to `wp-content/plugins/`, or zip it and upload it under Plugins > Add New.
2. Activate the plugin.
3. Go to Settings > ConsentFlow and enter your API URL and domain key.
4. Put `[consentflow_declaration]` on your cookie policy page.
